WHAT IS MISSING — DM, PLAYERS, ROUNDS, ENCOUNTERS
=================================================

This file is a gap analysis against the running app (not a roadmap with dates).
HOW-THE-TABLE-WORKS.txt and APP-HOW-IT-WORKS.txt describe what exists.
This file describes the table ceremonies that are thin or missing:
especially how a fight starts, how a round turns over, and how a fight ends.

Philosophy of the app (so “missing” is not “build a VTT”):
  Dice stay at the real table. The app keeps bookkeeping.
  It should still make the START / ROUND / FINISH loop obvious for DM and
  phones. Those loops are the gaps below.


HOW IT WORKS TODAY (SHORT)
--------------------------

ENCOUNTER START
  DM, at the hub: Start on a Ready template (map + at least one monster),
  or Resume a paused instance.
  That creates (or reopens) an encounter instance: round 1, turn slot 0,
  fog off and fully revealed, combatants in spawn order (monsters in the
  order they were added, then any player starts that were Saved).
  Initiative is 0 on everyone until the DM types numbers and hits Sort.
  Players cannot start, pause, or pick the template.

ROUNDS / TURNS
  “Whose turn” is currentTurnPosition in the initiative list.
  Round number goes up only when Next turn / End turn walks off the last
  combatant and wraps to the top.
  DM: Next turn on the tracker.
  Player: End turn on their own turn only (YOUR TURN panel).
  The creature who BECOMES current gets movement remaining reset to speed
  and turn chips (Action / Bonus / Reaction / Move) cleared.
  Dead, dying, and unconscious creatures are NOT skipped.

ENCOUNTER FINISH
  DM: Finalize → Won or Lost.
  Instance is marked completed (rewards only the first time).
  Session goes to a victory/defeat overlay.
  Players wait: “The DM is wrapping this fight.”
  DM then Return to table (hub) or Next encounter (picker).
  Table / Pause does not finalize: it keeps HP, positions, round, and turn.


1. ENCOUNTER START — WHAT IS MISSING
------------------------------------

DM
  No “about to start” step. Start is one click. There is no checklist:
    who is on the map, who is missing, fog on or off, surprise, init.
  Ready only means “map + monster.” Player starts are optional. Easy to
  go live with no PCs on the board.
  Template notes / objective / difficulty live on the hub notebook, not
  as a briefing card when combat opens.
  Fog starts off. There is no “start hidden” or “start in darkness.”
  Starting a different template while a fight is active marks the old
  instance completed without Won/Lost, overlay, XP, or loot.
  No surprise round. No “this side is surprised.”
  No rolling or entering initiative as a dedicated start phase — it is
  just fields on the tracker, all 0, first spawn order until Sort.
  Sort is initiative high-to-low only. Equal inits are not broken by
  Dex (or by a second d20). Order is whatever Sort’s stable ranking is.
  Players cannot type their own initiative; only the DM can.
  No “wait, phones ready?” — players already at the hub are yanked into
  combat when the DM hits Start.

PLAYERS
  Cannot start a fight, pick a template, or confirm they are placed.
  If they were not Place+Save on the template, they join combat with
  “You are not on the map yet” and must wait for DM + Name.
  No prompt to roll initiative and send it to the DM.
  No surprise / “you act this round / you don’t” flag on the phone.

STILL OK (BY DESIGN)
  Not auto-rolling init. The table rolls; someone types.
  Not a ready-check video call. Voice is at the real table.


2. ROUNDS — WHAT IS MISSING
---------------------------

There is no separate “round start” or “round end” screen. Round N is a
counter that ticks when the list wraps.

DM
  Next turn always advances one slot, including dead and unconscious
  creatures. You have to click through a corpse or skip them yourself
  (there is no Skip).
  Sort mid-fight jumps back to the top of the new order and does not
  change the round number. Easy to desync “we thought it was round 3.”
  No legendary-action economy at end of other turns (stat blocks can
  list legendary text; combat does not prompt it).
  No lair-action slot at initiative 20.
  No “start of round” list: concentration, poisoned, burning, dying,
  regeneration. Conditions are rings + a list; they do not tick.
  No Delay / Ready. You cannot park someone and come back later in the
  same round except by reordering arrows (which changes the list for
  everyone, not a one-off delay).
  Turn chips reset only for the person who just became current, not
  for the whole table at round wrap.
  Monster turns have no “End turn” of their own — the DM Next turn is
  that button. Easy to forget and leave the tracker on the goblin
  while you already moved a player.

PLAYERS
  End turn is allowed only on their turn. They cannot pass a monster
  or another PC.
  Header shows “Round N · Name’s turn” plus YOUR TURN / WAITING.
  There is no stronger “it’s you” (sound is out of scope; a bigger
  banner is still missing if the phone is face-down).
  They do not get a round-start reminder (e.g. “you are Poisoned —
  roll the d20 when the DM asks”).
  Movement remaining refreshes when their slot becomes current, which
  is correct, but they cannot see a “round wrapped” moment.

STILL OK (BY DESIGN)
  No auto-damage from poison. The DM asks for a d20; Save bar / prompt
  exists. Ticking conditions automatically would be a rules engine.


3. ENCOUNTER FINISH — WHAT IS MISSING
-------------------------------------

DM
  Only two outcomes: Won or Lost. No Flee, Retreat, Truce, or “left
  unfinished” besides Pause / Table.
  Finalize is a header button. Nothing in the tracker says “all enemies
  are at 0 HP — finalize?” You can sit in combat forever.
  XP from the template brief goes to EVERY character in the campaign,
  not only people who were in that fight.
  Loot is one text line appended to party loot, holder blank. No “give
  the gem to Elara.”
  HP, death-save state, and conditions on the encounter instance do not
  write a “after the fight” snapshot onto character sheets except HP
  that was already applied during attacks. No short rest / long rest.
  Next encounter from the overlay can start another fight without
  sitting at the hub; that is fine, but there is no rest reminder.
  Completing via “start the next template” (old instance set completed
  with no outcome) skips overlay and rewards.

PLAYERS
  Cannot Finalize. They stare at Victory/Defeat until the DM hits
  Return to table or Next encounter.
  They do not see a breakdown of XP per person or who got the loot
  besides later looking at the hub recap / party loot / Combat-tab XP.
  They cannot mark themselves stable, spend hit dice, or clear
  conditions as a rest — only the live combatant/sheet fields.

STILL OK (BY DESIGN)
  Not an inventory manager. Party loot as a notebook is enough if the
  DM types the line.
  Not auto-leveling from XP.


4. DM SIDE DURING COMBAT — EXTRA GAPS
-------------------------------------

  No remove-from-fight control besides never adding them. Late leavers
  stay in the turn order.
  No hidden initiative (players see Init N on the tracker).
  Concentration is not tracked (no “this caster is concentrating”).
  Cover, half cover, three-quarters, and high ground are not modeled
  (range is Chebyshev vs the attack’s feet only).
  AoE is “pick one target.”
  Spell slots on the sheet are not spent by casting; they are typed.
  Request save / Request reaction exist on the selected PC, not as a
  table-wide “everyone Dex save.”
  Combat notes (template brief, monster tactics) are not pinned on the
  live HUD; the DM leaves combat to read the hub notebook.
  Adding a monster mid-fight is “type a name, Enter” — no quantity,
  no start square picker (it drops by the spawn rules).
  Two different attack UIs: DM AttackBar at the bottom, player
  Action / End turn panel. Same math, different chrome.


5. PLAYER SIDE — EXTRA GAPS
---------------------------

  Cannot reveal/hide fog (correct for a player; they also cannot ask
  the app to ping the DM).
  Cannot drag anyone but themselves, and only on their turn.
  Help / Dash / Dodge / Hide / Disengage are declared into the activity
  feed. They do not change movement remaining or grant the Help bonus
  automatically.
  Death saves: they can enter a d20 when dying. They cannot stabilize
  someone else.
  Sheet drawer is separate from the map. Easy to miss attacks listed
  only on the sheet vs the turn panel.
  If the DM never hits Next turn after a player End turn, the table
  still matches; if the DM hits Next turn AND the player already did,
  the turn skips ahead — there is no lock. Double-advance is possible
  if both press close together.


6. HIGHEST-IMPACT GAPS (TABLE FLOW)
-----------------------------------

These are the holes you feel at a real session, in order:

  1. Start of fight is not a phase.
     Wanted: Start → who is on the map → type inits (PCs could submit)
     → Sort → round 1 begins. Optional: fog on, surprise skip.

  2. Initiative is DM-only and ties are sloppy.
     Wanted: each phone sends a d20; DM enters monster inits; Sort
     uses Dex (or a typed tie-break) when scores match.

  3. Next turn does not skip the dead.
     Wanted: Next / End turn lands on the next creature who can act,
     or a Skip control.

  4. Finish is easy to forget and rewards the whole campaign roster.
     Wanted: nudge when no standing enemies remain; XP only for PCs
     who were combatants; loot holder optional.

  5. Players cannot put themselves on the map.
     Wanted: “Join this fight” if the DM allows, or a DM prompt
     “place Finnie?” when they are at the table but not spawned.

  6. Pause vs Finalize vs Next-template is easy to mix up.
     Wanted: clearer copy. Table = pause, keep the fight.
     Finalize = the fight is over. Start next = only after overlay
     or from the hub, never silent-complete.

  7. No rest after the overlay.
     Wanted: optional “short rest” on the hub (hit dice typed, not
     auto-rolled) before the next Start.


7. DELIBERATELY NOT MISSING
---------------------------

Do not treat these as holes in this file. They are out of scope:

  Hex grids, lighting, sound, token animation, AI, OCR of photos,
  auto-rolling d20s, 5-10-5 diagonal, multiple maps in one fight,
  a full character builder, an external monster API, a rules engine
  that applies poison/concentration/legendary actions for you.


QUICK MAP: WHO CAN PRESS WHAT
-----------------------------

                    DM     Player
  Start template    yes    no
  Resume paused     yes    no
  Type initiative   yes    no (see only)
  Sort initiative   yes    no
  Next turn         yes    only on own turn (End turn)
  Skip dead         no     no
  Move any token    yes    own token, own turn
  Fog paint         yes    no
  + Name on map     yes    no
  Finalize W/L      yes    no (wait on overlay)
  Return to hub     yes    no
  Pause / Table     yes    no
  Leave table       —      yes (logs out)
